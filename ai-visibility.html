Solution 01

Become easier to find.

AI Visibility Strategy. Being the business that gets named when somebody asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Google for a business like yours. We correct the record those systems read — your listings, your schema markup, the words on your own pages — until it all says one consistent thing.

AI doesn’t know you exist, so it recommends someone else. Someone asks for the best business like yours in your area — and if you’re invisible to that system, you don’t exist to that customer, no matter how good you actually are.

01 — What this is

Be the answer, not a link in a list.

Solution 01 of 03 · the Be Found stage

We structure your content, your data and the signals around your business so that AI engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google’s AI answers — can find you, understand what you do, and recommend you correctly.

Most businesses have spent years on search rankings and have done nothing at all for how AI systems evaluate them. That is a real, current gap, and in most local markets nobody has closed it yet.

Two pieces from the journal go deeper if you want them: why a business is missing from AI answers, which is the seven causes in the order they occur, and what AI assistants can actually see about you, which is a ten-minute check you can run yourself before speaking to anyone.

What changes for you

  • Your business is described consistently everywhere a machine can read it, instead of three different ways across five places.
  • The questions your customers actually ask have answers on your own pages, in language a machine can quote.
  • You find out what AI currently says about you — which, for most businesses, is the part that stings.

Common questions

Isn’t this the same as search optimization?

Related, not the same. Traditional search gets you into a list of links a person clicks through. This is about being the answer itself — often without the person ever seeing a list of options at all.

How do we know if we’re visible already?

That’s the first thing worth checking, before assuming either way. Most businesses have never looked, and the answer is usually worse than they expect. It’s the first thing the audit does.

Is this a one-time fix?

No. The models change, and so do the places they source information from. This is ongoing work, not a single setup.
