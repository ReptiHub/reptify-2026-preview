How we work

01

We remove digital friction: the nameable points where a good business loses revenue and time between a customer wanting them and choosing them. A missed call at seven. A services page whose prices only exist inside an image. A record that disagrees with itself across four directories.

That work runs in four passes — audit, prioritise, implement, optimise — and the offers map onto the friction rather than onto a menu.

02

With an audit, and the audit is a conversation rather than a form. You get a written list of what is costing your business, ordered by what it costs, back within 24 to 72 hours. No obligation, and no report handed back with homework attached.

03

A web presence is typically four to six weeks from go-ahead to live. Smaller pieces are days rather than weeks. Ongoing work is ongoing by design and does not have an end date.

The variable is almost never us. It is content: photographs that exist, prices somebody is willing to publish, and the decisions only an owner can make about what the business says it does. Projects that run long run long there. We will tell you at the audit which parts of the timeline are ours and which are yours.

04

Three things. Access to what already exists — the site, the listings, the booking system, whatever is live. The facts nobody can invent for you: opening hours, what the services are actually called, and any price you are willing to see published. And one person with the authority to decide.

That last one sets the pace. The rest can be gathered without you; a decision cannot.

The order comes from what each gap costs you, not from what is most expensive to buy.

06

What it costs, and what you keep

08

It depends which pieces fit your business. Request an audit and we will walk through it based on what you actually need. We will not quote a number that is not built around your situation, and we do not publish a price list for work that is not the same twice.

09

You do. The hosting account, the domain and the code stay in your name, and you keep working software if you stop working with us. That is deliberate and it is the whole difference between owning a site and renting a page on somebody’s platform.

10

Whatever we agreed, written down before you pay for it. A site can be handed over complete and left alone, and it will keep working because it does not depend on us being there.

Some things genuinely need maintaining rather than finishing — how AI systems describe you changes because those systems change — and we will say plainly which category each piece of work is in rather than selling a retainer for something that is already done.

11

Some of it. A site that has been made legible stays legible. But AI models and the way they source information change continuously, so being described correctly is maintenance rather than a setup. We will be clear about which is which.

What we will not do

12

No, and nobody can. Rankings, placements, call volume and revenue all depend on your market, your business and the product decisions of companies neither of us controls. Anyone promising you otherwise is selling the guarantee rather than the work.

What the work does is remove the reasons you are being skipped. That is a smaller promise and it is the honest one.

13

Only real ones. A figure on this site is either first-hand and true or it is cited with a link to where it came from, and no review appears until a client has written it and approved it. Where we have nothing yet, the space stays empty rather than being filled with something plausible.

14

No. We run no affiliate or referral-commission programme and we do not buy, sell, rent or exchange contact lists. Where we approach a business, our own team researched it by hand, and research is not consent — nobody is texted because we found them. The terms say so in full.
