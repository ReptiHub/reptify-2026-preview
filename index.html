What happens
after you say yes.

  1. We go through your digital presence the way a customer does, and write down every place it loses you a customer. Audit
  2. The list gets ordered by what it is costing your business, so the expensive problems get fixed first — not the easy ones. Prioritize
  3. We remove the friction ourselves. Nothing is handed back to you as a report with homework attached. Implement
  4. We keep measuring as we go, because friction moves when a business does. Optimize

The loop comes round again, because friction moves when a business does. You see what each pass found before the next one starts.
