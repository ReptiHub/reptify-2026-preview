Where we work

We go and look first.

We work in person across the San Francisco Bay Area, Silicon Valley and the Napa Valley. A town already knows how to get a business in front of somebody walking past — signs, boards, windows, arrows. Our job is translating that to the web, and before we write about anywhere we do a photo walk: an hour on the pavement with a camera, because what a street does well is easier to show than to argue.
